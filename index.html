About Aolaina Biotechnology

A leading cosmetic manufacturer based in Guangzhou, China, dedicated to innovation, quality, and sustainable beauty solutions.

Innovating Beauty Through Science

Guangzhou Aolaina Biotechnology Co., Ltd. is a professional cosmetic manufacturer specializing in research, development, and production of high-end skincare and personal care products. Located in the Nansha District of Guangzhou, our state-of-the-art facility adheres to the highest industry standards.

We provide comprehensive OEM/ODM services for brands worldwide, offering customized formulations, packaging design, and full-scale production. Our commitment to quality is reflected in every product we create.
